# Run the local pilot and decide whether to expand

## Outcome

Validate that people actually meet and climb together before adding many more crags.

## Scope

- [ ] Invite roughly 10 to 20 climbers and arrange several voluntarily hosted sessions.
- [ ] Measure useful plans, accepted joins, self-reported completed outings, repeat use, and correction workload.
- [ ] Check actual VPS latency, export readability, model spend, backups, and moderation response.
- [ ] Review a four-week pilot against the documented goals and interview participants.
- [ ] Resolve wrong-location, access, and privacy issues before public expansion.
- [ ] Choose the next sector or area based on demand, permissions, field data, and maintenance ownership.
- [ ] Create a separate rope-climbing requirements issue before implementing Leda or Foster Falls planning.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pilot findings and an explicit continue, revise, or pause decision are documented.
- [ ] There are no unresolved material location, access, or privacy defects.
- [ ] Every proposed new area has a source plan and maintenance owner.
- [ ] Expansion is based on evidence rather than geographic coverage alone.
