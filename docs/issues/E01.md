# Confirm the pilot sector and local community needs

## Outcome

Choose a small first area that people can maintain, and test whether joinable climbing sessions solve the local problem.

## Scope

- [ ] Interview five to eight local climbers, including people who are new to Chattanooga.
- [ ] Choose one Stone Fort sector and identify two potential field reviewers.
- [ ] Identify the exact climbing area meant by St. Elmo before publishing it.
- [ ] Record the first supported grade ranges, session scenarios, and proposed success measures.
- [ ] Confirm the Crag Conditions URL with its owner; the supplied candidate page describes UK coverage.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A written scope names the pilot sector and community workflows.
- [ ] Interview findings distinguish real needs from assumptions.
- [ ] A person accepts responsibility for catalog corrections and community moderation.

## Dependencies

Can start independently.

Priority: P0. Milestone: M0 Data feasibility.
