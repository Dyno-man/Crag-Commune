## Outcome

Test whether joinable climbing sessions solve the local problem, and assess a maintainable path toward the owner's whole-Stone-Fort pilot goal.

## Scope

- [ ] Interview five to eight local climbers, including people who are new to Chattanooga.
- [x] Record all of Stone Fort as the owner's desired pilot coverage.
