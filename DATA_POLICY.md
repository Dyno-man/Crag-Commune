# Data and content policy

The repository's MIT license covers original software and documentation. It does not relicense third-party datasets, maps, photographs, guidebooks, forum posts, member profiles, private messages, or production database records.

## Proposed contribution model

Original public factual catalog contributions may be accepted under CC0 after the contributor explicitly agrees. Descriptive writing, photos, and maps require separate recorded terms. Member posts receive only the permissions needed to operate the service under the eventual member terms; they are not automatically an open dataset. Finalize these terms before public ingestion.

The proposed member-facing language and outstanding release decisions are in [the community notice draft](docs/planning/COMMUNITY_NOTICE_DRAFT.md). It is not a live site notice.

## Required source review

A source is publishable only after its owner, origin, evidence of rights, covered fields, permitted storage, public display, model processing, derivative map use, export rights, attribution, and expiration or withdrawal conditions are recorded. Unknown permission blocks ingestion. Owning a guidebook or linking to its author is insufficient evidence of reuse rights.

OpenBeta is a candidate with published CC0 climbing-data documentation; review the exact dataset and field provenance. Its photos and software have separate treatment. Mountain Project and theCrag are external references unless an applicable agreement grants the intended uses.

Keep restricted originals and permission correspondence in private controlled storage, not in git or public media. Public source registers should contain non-sensitive metadata and an internal evidence reference, not private correspondence.

## Correction and withdrawal

A reviewed correction creates a revision with provenance. A withdrawn source disables dependent facts and invalidates applicable plans, exports, search indexes, and model context. Retain the minimum audit history consistent with the agreement. Publicly downloaded snapshots cannot be recalled; stamp them with revision and date and link to current information.

No sensitive access coordinates are published merely because a source contains them. Review land-manager restrictions and the local publication context. Current official restrictions override community comments.
